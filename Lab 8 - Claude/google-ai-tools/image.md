# List of Free Google AI Tools

|---|---|---|
| [Pomelli](https://labs.google/pomelli) | Free AI marketing tool that builds your brand profile from your website and generates social content. | AI Marketing |
| [Stitch](https://stitch.withgoogle.com) | Turns prompts, sketches, and wireframes into clean, modern UI designs and frontend code. | AI UI Designer |
| [Opal](https://opal.google) | Build no-code AI mini-apps and multi-step workflows with simple language, like a free n8n. | No-Code Workflow Builder |
| [Antigravity](https://antigravity.google) | The "Cursor-Killer" agentic IDE that builds full apps from text prompts. | Agentic IDE |
| [Mixboard](https://labs.google/mixboard) | A mix of Canva and Pinterest where you generate and combine AI images into moodboards. | AI Moodboard Tool |
| [Disco](https://labs.google/disco) | Turns your open browser tabs into interactive AI apps with an AI-enabled pointer. | Web Discovery (Waitlist) |
| [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com) | Turns your docs, videos, and notes into summaries, podcasts, and study guides. | Research Assistant |
| [Learn Your Way](https://learnyourway.withgoogle.com) | Turns any topic into a personalized, AI-built course. | AI Learning Tool |
| [Flow Music](https://flowmusic.app) | Chat with an AI producer to generate full songs and music videos. | AI Music Studio |
| [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com) | The Lovable clone for prototyping MVPs and spinning up AI apps in seconds. | Vibe Coder |

| [Firebase Studio](https://firebase.studio) | A visual, AI-boosted cockpit for managing backend data and cloud logic. | Backend Mgmt |
| [Gemini Code Assist](https://codeassist.google) | Professional AI pair programmer integrated into VS Code, Cursor, and other IDEs, with a genuinely free tier. | Coding Extension |
